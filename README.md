Profile – Adejare Ezekiel Sanyaolu

Adejare Ezekiel Sanyaolu is a passionate and dedicated professional with a strong background in data science, research analysis, and operations research. With years of experience in both academic and applied settings, he specializes in data modeling, machine learning, statistical analysis, and decision-making tools. Ezekiel has worked across various sectors to extract insights from complex datasets and translate them into meaningful recommendations.

His academic journey includes a Master of Science in Operations Research, a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from the University of Lagos, and a diploma in Computer Engineering. He is currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Data Science and Information Technology and is constantly updating his knowledge to stay in tune with technological advancements.

Ezekiel is also a respected educator and facilitator, having delivered lectures and training on operations research, statistics, and management at institutions such as Rome Business School, Brazenwall Educational Centre, and the Federal College of Orthopaedic Technology in Lagos. Beyond analytics, he has a solid computer hardware and software foundation, gained from years of hands-on experience in IT and engineering roles.

Known for his analytical mindset, attention to detail, and commitment to continuous learning, Ezekiel enjoys exploring new ideas, mentoring others, and engaging in activities that blend technology with human-centered solutions. He loves travelling, reading motivational books, and experimenting with computer programming in his leisure time.
